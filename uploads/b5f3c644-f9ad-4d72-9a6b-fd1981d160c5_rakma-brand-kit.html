05 — Usage Rules

Do's and don'ts

These rules protect the brand equity. Follow them consistently across every touchpoint.

✓ DoUse on clean backgrounds
Rakma.

Always place the logo on white, off-white, ink, or solid brand colors. Never on patterns or busy images.

✗ Don'tChange the orange dot color
Rakma.

The orange period is non-negotiable. Never change it to blue, green, or any other color in the logo.

✓ DoInclude GLOBAL subtext where possible
Rakma.
GLOBAL

In headers, covers, and key touchpoints always include the GLOBAL wordmark underneath for full brand expression.

✗ Don'tStretch or distort
Rakma.

Never scale the wordmark non-proportionally. Always maintain the original aspect ratio.

✓ DoUse minimum size 16px
Rakma.
Rakma.

The minimum legible size is 16px on screen, 5mm in print. Never go smaller than this.

✗ Don'tAdd effects or shadows
Rakma.

Never add drop shadows, glows, outlines, or gradients to the wordmark. It should always be clean and flat.

08 — Voice & Tone

How Rakma speaks

Brand voice is as important as visual identity. Rakma's voice is human, direct, and ambitious — like a trusted friend who happens to be a millionaire giving you real advice.

Direct & Honest
Rakma speaks plainly. No jargon. No corporate fluff. Straight to the point, always. If something is good, say it's good. If something is hard, say it's hard.
Example
"Start with $100. Earn monthly rental income. No experience required. That's it."
Ambitious & Inspiring
Rakma believes everyone can build wealth. The tone is never condescending — it's the voice of a mentor who started from nothing and wants to help you do the same.
Example
"You don't need $200,000 to own real estate. You need $100 and the right platform."
Warm & Personal
Rakesh is the face. His immigrant story grounds everything. The brand should always feel like a person speaking — not a corporation broadcasting. Use first person often.
Example
"I built Rakma Global because I know what it feels like to be locked out. That ends now."
Write like this
✓ "Start investing from $100"
✓ "Earn monthly rental income"
✓ "I built this for people like me"
✓ "Real estate is no longer just for the wealthy"
✓ "12.4% projected annual yield"
✓ "Your money. Your property. Your future."
Never write like this
✗ "Leverage our proprietary fintech solutions"
✗ "We are pleased to offer you..."
✗ "Our cutting-edge disruptive platform"
✗ "Guaranteed high returns! Act now!"
✗ "Limited time offer — invest TODAY"
✗ "I am so blessed and grateful..."
